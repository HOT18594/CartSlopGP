Poly Haven assets used by Cart Slop GP / MEADOW LOOP
=====================================================

Source:  https://polyhaven.com
License: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0, public domain)
         https://polyhaven.com/license
Downloaded 2026-11-05 (1k JPG / 1k glTF variants).

TEXTURE SETS (assets/textures/polyhaven/)
  leafy_grass          grass_diff / grass_nor / grass_rough  (meadow ground)
  dirt_floor           dirt_diff / dirt_nor / dirt_rough     (race surface)
  bark_brown_02        bark_diff / bark_nor                  (deciduous trunks)
  pine_bark            pinebark_diff / pinebark_nor          (conifer trunks)
  island_tree_01       leaf_broad_* / branch_diff             (8-leaf broadleaf atlas)
  jacaranda_tree       leaf_diff / leaf_alpha / leaf_nor      (bipinnate frond atlas)
  grass_medium_01      grassblade_diff / grassblade_alpha     (grass card atlas)
  fern_02              fern_diff / fern_alpha                 (fern card atlas)

SCANNED GROUND-COVER MODELS (1k glTF, geometry + PBR textures)
  grass_medium_01, grass_medium_02, fern_02, moss_01, dead_tree_trunk_02

Only the small ground-cover models are shipped. Poly Haven's photogrammetry
TREE meshes (pine_tree_01, fir_tree_01, island_tree_01/02/03, jacaranda_tree,
etc.) are 40-950 MB of geometry each and are deliberately NOT included;
realistic trees are generated procedurally in-game from the CC0 bark and leaf
textures above (see scripts/minigames/realistic_tree.gd).

Derived RGBA foliage cards in assets/textures/foliage/ were produced by
tools/build_foliage_atlases.py from these CC0 sources. The dust/leaf particle
sprites are original works generated by the same script.

Reproduce the download with downloads/fetch_polyhaven.py (one folder above the
Godot project).
