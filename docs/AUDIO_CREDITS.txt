FORMAT NOTE (read before wiring)
--------------------------------
Kenney audio packs ship as .ogg (Ogg Vorbis) only. ffmpeg was not
available on the build machine, so the downloaded one-shots keep their
native .ogg format; Godot 4 imports .ogg natively. Wire these slots to
the .ogg names below (not .wav). The synthesized files are true .wav
(16-bit PCM mono, 22050 Hz).

SYNTHESIZED (procedural, in-house)
----------------------------------
- engine/engine_loop.wav : copy of assets/audio/engine_loop.wav;
  layered sines 110/220/330 Hz with 13 Hz wobble, 2.0 s seamless
  forward loop.
- engine/engine_boost.wav : procedural derivative of engine_loop.wav,
  resampled at 1.2x speed with linear interpolation (44100 -> 36750
  frames, 16-bit PCM mono, 22050 Hz). No external source.
- skid/skid_loop.wav : copy of assets/audio/skid_loop.wav;
  high-passed noise with circular crossfade, 1.4 s seamless loop.
- assets/audio/impact.wav and assets/audio/landing_thud.wav remain
  synthesized placeholders (noise burst + 70 Hz thump; 55/110 Hz
  decaying tones + rattle) and are superseded by the Kenney one-shots
  under assets/audio/sfx/.
