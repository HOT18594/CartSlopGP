# Sandbox arena layout

Generated by `tools/build_arena.py` into `scenes/dev/driving_test.tscn`.
Everything is metric and aligned to the 140 x 140 m pad (walls at +/-70 m).
Driving direction is **-Z**; the player spawns at `z = +66`.

## Rules the layout follows

- Lane width 10 m for a ~1.8 m vehicle, so two vehicles can pass.
- Ramps are 8 m wide (full lane) and 0.19 rad (~11 deg); the entry lip is
  buried so a CharacterBody can drive on without a step.
- Every jump has 4-6 m of gap and a 12 x 14 m landing pad.
- Prop spacing >= 3.5 m, so a 65 km/h pass has a clean line.
- Side zones (drift, slalom, field, cliff) are >= 18 m from the lane centreline
  so they never block the straight.

## Editing

Change the numbers in `tools/build_arena.py` and re-run:

```bash
python tools/build_arena.py
```

The generator writes the whole scene, including the HUD and pause menu
instances and the cart spawn.
