# AI image prompts

A small curated set of general backgrounds for the game. Copy any block below
straight into the image generator. Nothing here depends on a specific screen or
filename, so use them wherever they look good.

## The look

Every prompt repeats the same style on purpose. Keep it identical across all
images or the set will not feel like one game.

**Style:** flat 2D cartoon game illustration, thick dark navy outlines, chunky
rounded shapes, matte candy colors, cheerful arcade racing feel.

**Palette:** sky blue, cream, sunny yellow, pink, grass green, dark navy ink.

**Never:** text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, UI, buttons, photorealism,
3D render, glossy plastic, lens flare, realistic faces, thin linework, clutter.

## Prompts

Quiet all-rounder. Good for menus, loading and settings.

## Tips

- Generate 4 variations of one prompt, keep the best, then tweak one or two
  words. Do not rewrite the whole prompt between tries.
- Keep the middle of the frame quiet. Menus draw titles and buttons there.
- Ask for 16:9 for full-screen backgrounds, 1:1 for square cards.
- Send me the PNGs when you have them and I will wire them in with a fade. The
  existing hand-drawn backgrounds stay as the fallback.
